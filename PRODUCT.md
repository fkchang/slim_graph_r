# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Ruby gem producing SVG, with an optional StreamWeaver extension. Approved in docs/build-plan.md and the user's instruction to build.

## Users
StreamWeaver diagram authors, including agents writing compact Ruby descriptions.

## Product Purpose
Make editorial diagrams practical to author with short, semantic descriptions. Ruby owns placement and rendering; authors own content and relationships.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Architecture, flowchart, sequence, timeline, and hierarchical diagrams. Portable SVG and StreamWeaver documents. Accessible descriptions, light/dark themes, bounded graph complexity, no rendering network dependency. Efficiency claims require measurement.

## Brand Commitments
Use the diagram-design reference's restrained editorial visual direction.

## Evidence on Hand
Approved build plan and linked research. No measured token savings yet.

## Roadmap commitments
Full Diagram Design parity is the requested target, including its remaining diagram types and documented capabilities. Give Cathryn Lavery and Diagram Design visible inspiration credit. Preserve the independent Ruby DSL and measured token-efficiency story.
